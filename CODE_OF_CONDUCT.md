# Code of Conduct

## Scope and expectations

This policy applies to Craftmentation issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project-managed spaces. Participants should make technical collaboration welcoming
and respectful regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

- Discuss ideas and behavior with specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect differing experience levels and good-faith questions.
- Respect privacy, personal boundaries, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Give credit and correct mistakes when evidence warrants it.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate
intimidation, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Technical disagreement does
not justify personal attacks.

## Reporting

Project owner Barış Gündüz is responsible for handling conduct concerns. For behavior
that is already public, open an [issue](https://github.com/barisgunduz/craftmentation/issues)
with a minimal description and links to the public material, or ask for a private
reporting channel without identifying affected people or describing sensitive events.

No dedicated confidential conduct-reporting channel is currently documented. Do not
post private correspondence, identifying information, or sensitive allegations in a
public issue. Wait for an agreed private route before sharing that material. If a
report concerns the project owner, no independent project appeal channel is currently
available; participants may use the hosting platform's reporting tools.

## Maintainer response

The project owner may clarify expectations, request corrections, remove inappropriate
content, limit participation, or exclude participants according to severity and repetition.
Reports should be reviewed fairly, with only necessary information shared. Participants
may ask for clarification or reconsideration through an agreed reporting route.
No guaranteed response time or independent review body is established.
